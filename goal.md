SELECTION LOGIC:

For each album, weigh three factors:

1. Cultural proximity — does the album belong to a lineage, scene, or ecosystem the user is genuinely connected to?
2. Aesthetic alignment — texture, rhythm, structure, emotional tone
3. Critical intensity — how strongly is it endorsed, and by whom?

Important rules:
- Cultural proximity is the PRIMARY factor for Core. Aesthetic alignment alone is not sufficient.
- High critical intensity can elevate a weak-match album into Bridge or Exploration, but NOT into Core.
- Do NOT over-prioritize familiarity or safe picks.
- Do NOT ignore culturally important releases even if stylistically distant.
- Source diversity matters: a candidate from The Wire or RA carries different cultural weight than a Pitchfork BNM. Do not let any single source dominate the final selection.
- Pitchfork BNM should not account for more than 2–3 Core or Bridge picks.
