# Taste Graph

## Core Cultural Affinities

The user is primarily drawn to music situated at the intersection of:

- **Spiritual and exploratory jazz lineages**  
  (Pharoah Sanders, Sun Ra, Don Cherry → contemporary continuations)

- **Afro-diasporic and global rhythmic traditions**  
  (West African, Ethiopian, Latin American, Caribbean, and their reinterpretations)

- **Beat-driven and jazz-informed electronic scenes**  
  (Brainfeeder / LA beat continuum, experimental hip-hop, neo-soul hybrids)

These form a continuous space where:
- rhythm is cultural and structural
- improvisation meets production
- tradition is reworked through contemporary forms

---

## Extended Cultural Field (Important)

Beyond core affinities, the user consistently engages with a wider cultural spectrum:

- **Soul, funk, and political black music traditions**  
  (Nina Simone, Curtis Mayfield, SAULT, etc.)

- **Dub, reggae, and sound system lineage**  
  (Rhythm & Sound, roots reggae, dub aesthetics)

- **Global archival / ethnographic recordings**  
  (traditional, field recordings, cross-cultural documents)

- **Chanson, European songwriting, and classic pop**  
  (Brassens, Hardy, Gainsbourg, etc.)

- **American songbook, jazz standards, and historical recordings**

These are not nostalgic preferences, but part of a broader interest in:
> **music as cultural document and transmission**

---

## Secondary Affinities

- **UK post-dubstep and introspective electronic**  
  → space, fragmentation, emotional restraint

- **Textural / design-oriented electronic lineage**  
  → Warp-adjacent, minimal, ambient, experimental

- **Minimal / systems-based composition**  
  → Reich, Glass, Moondog, krautrock (CAN, NEU!)

---

## Experimental / Edge Zones

The user regularly touches:

- free improvisation  
- noise-adjacent or abstract electronics  
- avant-garde composition  
- hybrid contemporary classical  

But typically prefers:
> **when these retain some physical, rhythmic, or emotional anchor**

---

## Filtered Songwriting Affinity

Strong connection to:

- melancholic, understated songwriting  
  (The Smiths, Elliott Smith, Nick Drake, etc.)

This reflects:
- emotional precision
- avoidance of rhetorical or exaggerated expression

Not a primary discovery axis, but a recurring anchor.

---

## Aesthetic Tendencies

Across all domains, the user responds to:

- rhythmic intelligence over raw intensity  
- texture and timbre as compositional elements  
- repetition with variation  
- spatial depth and production detail  
- hybrid acoustic/electronic approaches  
- emotional restraint and ambiguity  
- gradual evolution rather than dramatic contrast  

They are drawn to music that feels:
- **situated in a context or lineage**
- **intentional and constructed**
- **immersive rather than declarative**

---

## Cultural Orientation

- Strong interest in **scenes, ecosystems, and lineages**
- Values **cross-cultural connections**
- Engages with music as:
  - cultural artifact
  - historical continuum
  - evolving system

The user often connects distant domains intuitively:
(e.g. spiritual jazz ↔ beat scene ↔ post-dubstep ↔ minimalism)

---

## Soft Aversions

Tends to disengage from:

- bombastic or overly anthemic music  
- generic or identity-less indie/pop  
- purely functional dancefloor music  
- overt sentimentality  
- purely conceptual or academic work without embodiment  
- revivalism without reinterpretation  

---

## Openness and Exploration

- Open to **unexpected or unfamiliar material**
- Especially receptive to:
  - culturally significant releases
  - strongly endorsed albums
  - works that reveal new scenes or lineages

Exploration is important, but should:
- connect loosely to existing sensibilities
- or be justified by clear cultural or critical relevance

---

## Interpretation Guidance

This profile represents **a broad cultural field with strong centers of gravity**, not a narrow taste.

When evaluating music:

- Think in terms of:
  - lineages
  - scenes
  - cultural relationships

- Allow:
  - weak matches if culturally important
  - unexpected links across domains

- Prioritize:
  - intersection
  - context
  - identity

- Avoid reducing this profile to:
  - genres
  - surface similarity
